Radio

Radio turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a pocket internet radio. It joins a 2.4 GHz WiFi network, plays stations from around the world from Radio Garden's map, MP3 and AAC (HE-AAC included), and lets you play the radio with punch-in effects on the keys.

Version 1.3. Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

  • Install: from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
  • Try it in the browser: the stations play, with the effects on the keys, through Radio's own decoders.
  • Manual: joining a network, the knobs, favorites, the effects, the synth, recording over USB.
  • Download radio-1.3.fwsc: for the command-line installer, python3 base/tools/fm1_install.py radio-1.3.fwsc.
  • Source: MPL-2.0. Built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp, from Felucca by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments.
